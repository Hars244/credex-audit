## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes, completely free. No credit card. No trial
period. No "freemium" restrictions. You get the
full audit instantly.

**Q: Do you store my spending data?**
A: We store the tools and amounts you enter to
generate your shareable report URL. We never sell
this data. Your email (if provided) is only used
to send your report and — for high-savings cases —
to have a Credex team member reach out with options.

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: Every recommendation is based on publicly listed
pricing from vendor websites, verified during
submission week. We cite our sources in PRICING_DATA.md.
The logic is rule-based — not AI-generated — so a
finance person can read and verify every calculation.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI credits from companies
that overforecast their usage. If SpendScan finds
significant savings opportunities for your team,
Credex may be able to help you save even more beyond
what plan optimisation alone can achieve.

**Q: What if my tools are not listed?**
A: We currently support Cursor, GitHub Copilot,
Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Windsurf — the six
most commonly used AI tools in engineering teams.
If you use something not listed, run the audit for
the tools you do use and note the gap in the comments
when you submit your email.
