# Unit Economics — SpendScan

## What is a Converted Lead Worth to Credex?

Credex sells discounted AI credits. A company spending
$500/month on AI tools retail might buy $400/month
in Credex credits — saving $100/month while Credex
earns margin on the difference.

**Assumptions (conservative):**
- Average deal size: $300/month in credits purchased
- Gross margin on credits: 15% (Credex buys surplus
  credits at discount and resells)
- Monthly revenue per customer: $300 × 15% = $45
- Average customer lifetime: 12 months
- LTV per customer: $45 × 12 = $540

One converted customer = **$540 LTV**

This is conservative. High-value customers (spending
$2,000+/month on AI tools) could represent $3,000-
$5,000 LTV.

## Conversion Funnel Math

1,000 audits completed
→ 200 email captures (20% conversion)
→ 30 consultation requests (15% of email captures)
→ 6 credit purchases (20% of consultations)
→ LTV: 6 × $540 = $3,240 revenue per 1,000 audits

**Revenue per audit: $3.24**

This needs to be at least $2-3 per audit to justify
building and maintaining the tool. We're at the
lower bound — improving email capture rate or
consultation booking rate has high leverage.

If email capture → consultation improves to 25%:
1,000 audits → 200 emails → 50 consultations →
10 purchases → $5,400 revenue per 1,000 audits

That's $5.40 per audit — highly profitable.

## What Needs to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

**$1M ARR = $83,333/month in recurring credit revenue**

At $45/month per customer:
**$83,333 / $45 = 1,852 active customers needed**

**Path to 1,852 customers in 18 months:**

Month 1-3: Organic launch
- 3,000 audits/month via HN, Reddit, Credex list
- 600 email captures/month
- 90 consultations/month
- 18 new customers/month
- Running total: 54 customers

Month 4-9: Content flywheel
- Audit results get shared → organic growth
- 8,000 audits/month
- 1,600 email captures/month
- 240 consultations/month
- 48 new customers/month
- Running total: 54 + (48×6) = 342 customers

Month 10-18: Paid acquisition kicks in
- Now have LTV data to justify $83 CAC on LinkedIn
- Combine paid + organic for 15,000 audits/month
- 3,000 email captures/month
- 450 consultations/month
- 90 new customers/month
- Running total at month 18: 342 + (90×9) = 1,152

Shortfall: 700 customers.

**What needs to be true to close the gap:**
1. Email capture rate must be 25%+ not 20%
2. Consultation → purchase must be 25%+ not 20%
3. Average deal size must be $400/month not $300
4. Customer lifetime must be 18 months not 12

All four are achievable but none are guaranteed.
The most leveraged improvement is the consultation
booking flow — adding a Calendly embed directly
in the high-value email could 2x that conversion.

**The $1M ARR scenario is plausible but requires
at least 2 of the 4 improvements above.**
